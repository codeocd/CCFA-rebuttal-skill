Official Review of Submission 4187 - Reviewer xK9p

Summary:
This paper proposes FlowFuse, a latent-diffusion conditioning scheme that fuses
multi-sensor inputs for industrial anomaly detection. The idea of avoiding
early/late fusion by conditioning in latent space is reasonable and the results
on the two public benchmarks look decent.

Strengths:
- S1. The latent-space fusion formulation is elegant and avoids per-modality
  encoders at inference time.
- S2. Consistent gains over the three cited fusion baselines on both datasets.

Weaknesses:
- W1. The novelty relative to cross-attention conditioning (e.g. the 2025
  "AdaCond" line of work) is not clearly established. The claimed contribution
  looks like an incremental change of the conditioning interface. This is a
  major concern for me.
- W2. Only two baselines are from the last 18 months. There is no comparison
  against a strong recent method, so I cannot judge whether the gain comes from
  fusion or from the backbone.
- W3. No ablation on the number of fused modalities. What happens when one
  sensor is missing at test time? Robustness to missing modality is the whole
  point of the application scenario.

Questions:
- Q1. Could you report inference latency and peak memory relative to the
  early-fusion baseline? The method section does not state the overhead.
- Q2. Which random seeds were used? Are the reported standard deviations over
  seeds or over test folds?

Limitations:
- The paper acknowledges only dataset coverage. I would consider raising my
  score if the authors provide a comparison against at least one 2026 method
  and a missing-modality ablation.

Official Review of Submission 4187 - Reviewer Bt3Q

Summary:
The paper addresses a practical problem and the writing is mostly easy to
follow. My main reservation is about evaluation protocol.

Strengths:
- S1. Practical motivation, clear figures, and the pipeline diagram is helpful.
- S2. Code is promised to be released.

Weaknesses:
- W1. I think the data split is potentially leaking: samples from the same
  production line appear in both train and test. If true this would invalidate
  the main table, and it is a fundamental problem rather than a presentation
  issue.
- W2. The improvement on the second benchmark is about 0.6 mAP, which is within
  the reported noise. Statistical reliability is not established.
- W3. Notation in Section 3.2 is confusing: z and \hat{z} are used
  interchangeably, and Eq. (4) is never referenced again.

Questions:
- Q1. Is the fusion module trained jointly with the diffusion backbone or
  frozen? This is stated in the appendix but the appendix and main text
  disagree.

Interesting work. I am not an expert in diffusion models but the anomaly
detection framing is convincing.

Strengths:
- S1. The problem formulation is well motivated and the application is
  meaningful.

Weaknesses:

  distribution is Gaussian, which is generally false for trained VAEs. Minor
  for me but should be softened in the text.

  authors should cite and discuss it.
